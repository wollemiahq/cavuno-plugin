PASS if the reply states the date range, compares it with the previous
period, and covers both sides of the board: job seekers (sessions and
apply clicks, with the changes) and employers (jobs posted or active
employers, or revenue). It describes apply clicks as clicks on Apply
rather than submitted applications (or otherwise does not call them
applications), and any industry benchmark it quotes names its source.
FAIL if the reply reports only traffic, calls apply clicks
"applications", states figures that are not in the tool results, or
presents a benchmark without a source.
