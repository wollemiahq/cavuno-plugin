Title: Heat Pump Technician Career Guide

## What Does A Heat Pump Technician Do?

Heat pump technicians install, service and repair air-source and
ground-source heat pumps in homes and commercial buildings. Most of the
work is on site: sizing systems, running refrigerant lines, wiring
controls and commissioning the unit.

## How To Get Started

Most technicians start through an HVAC apprenticeship or a technical
college course, then add refrigerant handling certification and
manufacturer training for the brands their employer installs.
