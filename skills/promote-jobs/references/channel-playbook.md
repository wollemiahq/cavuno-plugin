# Channel playbook

## Pick the side you are promoting to

A job board has two audiences. Decide which one a piece of promotion is
for before choosing the channel and the measure.

| Audience | Goal | Good channels | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Job seekers | Visits that lead to apply clicks and alert signups | Newsletter, LinkedIn roundups, niche communities, X job posts | Apply clicks and alert signups per campaign |
| Employers | Postings and renewals | Direct email, LinkedIn prospecting, posts that share board results | Employer inquiries and paid posts |

Employers are usually the harder side to win and keep; promotion aimed at
job seekers is also proof for employers when you report its results.

## Cadence

- Start with one platform and a consistent weekly rhythm; the social-media
  post suggests committing to one platform for 90 days and letting tagged
  referral traffic decide whether to continue.
- Repurpose one job list across surfaces: a LinkedIn roundup, per-job X
  posts, a newsletter section.
- Keep a consistent weekly send for newsletters so subscribers know when to
  expect it.

## Content beyond job lists

Mix job posts with content people value when they are not job hunting:
salary information already on the board's jobs, hiring trends visible in
the week's postings, notable employers. The social-media post suggests
most posts should build community value rather than only promote jobs.

## Bring people back

- Remind employers when their posts expire and when seasonal hiring starts.
- Point job seekers to job alerts so they return when they next need the
  board.

## Get the board listed

When the user asks where to promote the board itself:

1. Make sure job pages are eligible for Google for Jobs (structured data;
   see Cavuno's job board SEO docs).
2. Submit to job board directories that fit the niche, free ones first.
3. Add aggregators (for example Indeed organic, Jooble) once the board has
   steady inventory.
4. Tag each listing's link and compare referral traffic before paying for
   more.

## After a campaign

Hand measurement to the `review-performance` skill: traffic by campaign
(`/analytics/traffic`), apply clicks by job and channel
(`/analytics/apply-clicks`).
