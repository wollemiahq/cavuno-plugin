# Pricing guidance

Use this only when the user asks what to charge or how to structure plans.
Cavuno does not prescribe a market price; treat every figure as the
source's suggestion and say where it comes from.

## Choose what to sell first

- Start with one measurable offer: a public pay-per-post plan. Add featured
  listings once there is traffic, and subscriptions once employers return
  regularly.
- Keep pay-per-post next to subscriptions: new and occasional employers try
  pay-per-post; regular posters save with a subscription.
- Use the fewest tiers that reflect genuinely different employer needs. The
  employer-subscriptions guide suggests two to three to start.
- Reserve some extras for upsells (one-off featured placement, sponsorships,
  recruitment services) rather than putting everything in a subscription.
- Charge job seekers only when the listings are curated or exclusive and the
  board already has a loyal audience; otherwise monetize employers first.

## Associations and member rates

- Run member and non-member rates as a membership, not a coupon: the member
  rate shows on every plan card and applies at checkout, and membership can
  include posts or make posting a member benefit.
- The pricing-models post suggests non-members pay a 30 to 50% premium over
  member rates. Present it as that post's suggestion.

## Set a price

- Price from the audience and results the board can show: relevant
  applicants, apply clicks, repeat purchases, employer feedback. Traffic
  alone does not establish pricing power.
- Write the first price down as a hypothesis with the measure that will
  confirm or reject it.
- Present price against the employer's cost of an unfilled role or a hire,
  not only per post.

## Test and change prices

- Test only on new employers; do not change existing customers' prices
  mid-contract. A Cavuno price change affects future purchases only.
- Judge a test on revenue per visitor as well as conversion rate; low
  transaction volume needs a long test.
- Before a price rise: the pricing-models post suggests at least 60 days'
  notice and honoring existing rates for six to twelve months. Lead the
  announcement with the value delivered, then what changes and when.
- Seasonal premiums or regional discounts can be run with plans or
  promotion codes; keep campaigns in codes, standing benefits in memberships.

## Discounts

- Use promotion codes for launches, seasons, partner deals and
  win-backs. Set an expiry and a redemption limit, and retire the code when
  the campaign ends.
- Restrict a coupon to the plans it is meant for at creation; the
  restriction cannot be edited afterward.

## Other revenue to mention only when relevant

Newsletter sponsorships, display ads (AdSense, set up in the dashboard),
affiliate partnerships (disclose them, and compare the provider's accepted
actions with Cavuno outbound clicks), employer branding and service plans.
